## 🚀 Projects

### 📊 [User Activity & Email Engagement Optimization](https://github.com/IhorPetryshyn/SQL-Analysis-Project)
* **Business Questions:** How are user activity and email dispatches distributed geographically across different countries? What are the optimal dispatch intervals to maintain an active subscriber base without increasing unsubscribe rates?
* **Business Insights:**
  * Identified core geographic markets based on verified user accounts (leading countries: Italy, Singapore, Germany).
  * Consolidated data via CTEs and `UNION ALL` to precisely calculate the ratio between open/click rates and unsubscribes, enabling optimized email dispatch schedules that preserve subscriber retention.

### 📈 [Global Email Campaign Performance Tracking](https://github.com/IhorPetryshyn/Tableau-Project)
* **Business Questions:** How effectively do marketing email campaigns convert users? How can stakeholders be provided with a real-time BI tool to track Open Rate, Click Rate, and CTOR across different countries?

### 🛍️ [E-Commerce Customer & Sales Analytics](https://github.com/IhorPetryshyn/E-Commerce-Customer-Sales-Analytics-Project)
* **Business Questions:** What are the primary revenue drivers (traffic channels, device types, geographic regions)? What is the business impact and LTV difference between subscribed vs. non-subscribed users?
* **Business Insights:**
  * **Desktop accounts for ~60% of total revenue**, while Mobile generates **38%**. Given rapid growth in mobile traffic, optimizing mobile UX/UI was highlighted as top priority #1.
  * Discovered strong channel synergy ($r > 0.81$): Paid Search actively stimulates Organic Search, which subsequently converts into Direct visits.
  * Subscribed users drive consistent long-term LTV ($r = 0.650$), whereas unsubscribed users are crucial for tactical revenue spikes during promotional campaigns.

### 🧪 [Automated A/B Testing: Conversion Rate Optimization Across Traffic Channels](https://github.com/IhorPetryshyn/Complex-Project-2)
* **Business Questions:** Does the new UI configuration/algorithm (Variant B) improve checkout funnel conversion rates compared to the baseline (Variant A) across various devices and acquisition channels?
* **Business Insights:**
  * **Test 1 (Success):** Mobile version demonstrated a **+17.14% lift** at the payment information entry stage (`add_payment_info`). Recommended deploying Variant B across Desktop and Mobile (covering 98% of total traffic).
  * **Risk Prevention:** Automated Z-test evaluation ($\alpha = 0.05$) detected a sharp conversion drop in Organic Search traffic (down to -19.46%). This insight prevented a harmful rollout and safeguarded organic revenue streams.
